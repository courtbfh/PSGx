## PSGx

Precise, Small, and Genuinely Xenodochial, this miniature hardware timer combines vintage aesthetics with tactile analog controls. It distills the classic Pomodoro technique down to its purest, most satisfying physical form. Every focus session starts with a satisfying turn of a dial rather than a digital tap. It sits quietly on your desk, offering a warm orange glow that keeps you on track without distractions.
